Frequently Asked Questions

How does rodizio-style dining work at Manolo's?

Rodizio is all-you-can-eat, priced per person. Servers walk the dining room with skewers of freshly grilled beef, pork and chicken and slice portions onto your plate. Keep eating as long as you like and try a bit of everything. (Shrimp isn't part of the rodizio.)

Can I walk in, or do I need a reservation?

Both work. Walk-ins are seated whenever a table is open. For weekends or larger groups, reserve ahead with the form above or call us at (516) 774-5573.

Which grilled meats are on the menu?

Picanha (top sirloin), skirt steak, New York steak, lamb chops, pork chops, spare ribs, chicken breast and rotisserie chicken, plus Portuguese chouriço. Most plates come with rice and sides such as black beans and sweet plantain.

What is the dining experience like?

A relaxed, family-friendly dining room on Merrick Road with tableside rodizio service and parking on site. It works just as well for a weeknight dinner as for a celebration.

What seafood do you serve?

Clams, mussels, baby octopus, shrimp, lobster, branzino, salmon, sole and bacalhau (cod), along with paella marinheira, paella valenciana and mariscada in green sauce.

Is Manolo's a good fit for families with kids?

Yes. We're kid-friendly, and the kids menu has mozzarella sticks and chicken fingers with fries for younger diners.
